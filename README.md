Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba`.

`main.ts` writes a two-part unsent micro-confession — a `To:` line naming
who it was meant for and the unsent message itself, one line written exactly
as it was typed — to `/output/entry.txt`. The entry is validated before it is
written: 1 to 260 characters in total, the message line at most 200
characters, exactly two lines, not only white space.

The entry:

> To: my old manager, who kept the layoff list in a spreadsheet
> I knew my name was on it three weeks before you told me, and I still rehearsed looking surprised.
